Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit rude-beat.com. Short version: we don't ask you for anything, we don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we don't build profiles. We use one analytics tool — and only if you allow it.

01Who is responsible

Data controller

Wojciech Warmuz — operator of Rude Beat Distro.
Contact: contact@rude-beat.com

Write to that address for anything related to your data — access, deletion, objections or just questions.

02What we collect and why

There are no forms, no accounts and no newsletter on this site, so we never receive your name, e-mail or any details you type. What does get processed is the following:

Data Purpose Legal basis (GDPR)
Server logs — IP address, browser type, date and time of the request Keeping the site online, security, diagnosing failures Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in running a working, secure site
Analytics — pseudonymous identifier, pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location (city level), referring source, clicks on Spotify / YouTube links Understanding which releases and links people actually open, so we know what to publish next Art. 6(1)(a) — your consent, given in the cookie banner
Your cookie choice, stored in your browser under rbd-consent Remembering whether you accepted or rejected analytics, so we stop asking Art. 6(1)(f) — necessary to respect the choice you made

We do not use your data for automated decisions that produce legal effects for you, and we do not target advertising. This site is not directed at children.

03Cookies and similar storage

Before you make a choice in the banner, no analytics cookies are set. We use Google Consent Mode, which starts with analytics storage denied and only switches on if you press “Accept”.

Name Set by Purpose / lifetime
rbd-consent This site (localStorage) Stores your cookie decision. Kept until you clear your browser storage.
_ga, _ga_<ID> Google Analytics — only after consent Distinguishes visitors so visits can be counted. Up to 2 years.

You can change or withdraw your decision at any time by clicking “Cookies” in the footer of the site. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened while consent was active.

04Third parties and embedded content

Some parts of the page are loaded from external services. When your browser fetches them, it sends your IP address to those services — this happens as part of loading the page, independently of the analytics consent above:

  • YouTube — release thumbnails and the embedded player in the “Latest transmission” section. The player is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com, which does not set tracking cookies until you actually start playback.
  • Google Analytics — loaded with consent denied by default, as described above.

Both are provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, in particular to the United States; Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses as safeguards. Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The typefaces used across the site are served from our own server rather than from Google Fonts, so simply opening a page does not disclose your IP address to a font provider.

The site is hosted on infrastructure of our hosting provider, which processes server logs on our behalf. If you open a Spotify or YouTube link from this site, you leave it and the privacy policy of that platform applies from that point on.

05How long we keep data

  • Server logs — for the period applied by our hosting provider, typically a few weeks up to several months.
  • Analytics data — for the retention period configured in our Google Analytics property, no longer than 14 months.
  • Your cookie choice — stays in your browser until you clear it.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data deleted,
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw consent at any time — via the “Cookies” link in the footer,
  • lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.

To exercise any of these, write to contact@rude-beat.com. Note that analytics data is pseudonymous — we usually cannot link it back to a specific person, so for some requests we may need extra information to identify the data in question.

Providing data is not a legal or contractual requirement. You can browse the entire site with analytics rejected — nothing stops working.

07Changes to this policy

If we add a new tool or change how the site works, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes affecting consent will be signalled by showing the cookie banner again.
